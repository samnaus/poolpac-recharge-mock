Hydrotherapy developed with physiotherapists

Passion Spas has spent years working closely with physiotherapists and masseurs to design massage experiences you won't find in other spas. The Recharge brings three of them home, so you've got a masseur on call 24/7 who keeps going as long as you like.

Soft Tissue Massage™

Designed to mimic the therapeutic effect of a professional massage, Soft Tissue Massage™ focuses only on muscles and connective tissue, relaxing and rejuvenating them.

The number one thing masseurs tell Passion Spas is: only massage the muscle and connective tissue. That's why every jet in a Passion Spa is positioned the way it is, and why many masseurs rate Passion Spas number one for hydrotherapy.

Lumbar Support™

Everyone has a natural hollow in the lower back, the lumbar zone. Passion Spas' design team worked with professionals from the recliner industry to perfect the shape of the shell so it supports that curve and eases pressure on the lower spine.

It works hand in hand with the jets for targeted relief of the lower back muscles, and even sitting dry in the seat, you'll notice the comfort compared with other spas.

Freestyle Massage Option

Unique to Passion Spas, Freestyle Massage puts you in control. Rotate and guide the jet to your shoulder, hip, knee or ankle, even above the waterline, and the vibrating warm water works the muscle.

It's especially handy for localised tension or soreness, because you choose both where the massage goes and how strong it is.

The wider Passion Spas range also offers therapies such as Aqua Rolling Massage™, Intense Therapy Zone™, Levitation Bed™, Neck Relief Massage™, Waterfall Massage™ and Therapy Wave Zone™ on selected models. These aren't part of the Recharge; ask us which spa suits the therapy you're after.

Planet friendly

Award-winning Hybrid Heating™

While you're enjoying your Recharge, you're also looking after your power bill and the environment. Passion Spas are designed to cut energy use through a range of features, led by award-winning Hybrid Heating™.

Hybrid Heating™ technology supplements the heat produced by the 3 kW electric heater. Specially designed massage pumps agitate the water molecules through friction, which generates heat and eases energy use by the heater.

Programmable controls let you set when the spa runs, and the low-wattage filtration pump (200 W) uses only a small amount of electricity to keep the water circulating through the filter. Triple-layer insulation, an extra-thick walk-on safety cover and Everlast™ floor support wrap the spa in one of the most effective heat-containment designs available.

Perfect water

Amazingly clear water

Passion Spas use a multi-faceted purification approach that helps keep the water crystal clear and cuts down on extra sanitising and maintenance. On the Recharge, that means 100% filtration plus ozone sanitation, including a water ozone mixer.

A dedicated filtration pump draws water through the 50-square-foot spa filter, so the water is filtered whether or not the massage pumps are running. The Recharge also has a high-volume side-load skimmer, gravity floor drain, external drain and two hi-flow suctions.

UV purification, which completes Passion Spas' full Synergy Water Purification System, is part of the Signature and Exclusive Collections. The Recharge is a Pure Collection spa.
