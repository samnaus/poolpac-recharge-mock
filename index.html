Efficiency

Award-winning Hybrid Heating™

Hybrid Heating™ technology supplements the heat produced by the 3 kW electric heater. Specially designed massage pumps agitate the water molecules through friction, which generates heat and eases energy use by the heater.

Programmable controls let you set when the spa runs, and the low-wattage filtration pump (200 W) uses only a small amount of electricity to keep the water circulating through the filter. Triple-layer insulation, an extra-thick walk-on safety cover and Everlast™ floor support help contain the heat.

Water care

Clear water, with ozone purification

The Pure Collection is equipped with an ozone purification system, including a water ozone mixer, to help keep the water clean and reduce the need for extra sanitation.

A dedicated filtration pump draws water through the 50-square-foot spa filter, so the water is filtered whether or not the massage pumps are running. The Recharge also has a high-volume side-load skimmer, gravity floor drain, external drain and two hi-flow suctions.

Design

Seating comfort, in a classic round form

The Recharge is a classically styled round spa. Seat contours and depths are designed for comfort, with a variety of seating positions and room for different body lengths. Controls sit within easy reach, and the shell lines provide locations for lighting, jets and features.

LED lighting and aromatherapy are included. Cabinet finishes shown on this page are official Passion Spas product photography; talk to Poolpac about the finish available for your install.

Construction

Designed to perform and built to last

The acrylic shell is reinforced with vinyl-ester resin laminates, applied by hand, and supported by a durable synthetic structure engineered for water exposure and outdoor conditions. The entire base is enclosed with Everlast™ synthetic floor support, providing integral seat supports and structural integrity.

High-density urethane insulation and ultra-efficient dual-wrap cabinet insulation are standard, along with a synthetic maintenance-free cabinet and four removable side panels.
